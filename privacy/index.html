English

21Count is a blackjack and card-counting practice game. It does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data.

  • No account or sign-in. No advertising, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs.
  • The app never connects to the internet.
  • Your game progress and settings are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage, and are deleted when you delete the app. You can also erase them at any time in Settings → About & privacy → Reset all progress.
  • The app requests no permissions (no camera, location, contacts, microphone or photos).
  • There is no betting of any kind: no chips, no real money, no purchases and no prizes. Points are earned only for right decisions.
  • The app is not directed at children.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
