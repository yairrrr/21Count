Frequently asked questions

Is this real gambling? No. All chips are virtual. There is no real money, no purchases and no prizes. 21Count is for learning and entertainment.

Do I need an account or internet? No. The app works completely offline and stores progress only on your device.

How do I start over? Settings → About & privacy → Reset all progress. Your language and table settings are kept.

Is card counting legal? Counting in your head is not cheating, but casinos are private businesses and may ask counters to stop playing or leave.

Which rules does the table use? 1–8 decks (default 6), blackjack pays 3:2, dealer stands or hits soft 17 (your choice), double after split, up to four split hands, late surrender, insurance 2:1.
